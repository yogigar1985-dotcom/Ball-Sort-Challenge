This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile application Color Sort Master (Package: com.aistudio.colorsortmaster.pxqyt) created and published by Yogesh Garud.

1. Overview

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Color Sort Master is designed as an offline-first casual puzzle game. We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal credentials (such as your legal name, physical address, or phone number) to play the game.

2. Information Collection and Use

A. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect or store your personal identification details (e.g., name, national identity numbers, passwords).
  • We do not collect biometrics, contacts, microphone, camera, or sensitive personal data.
  • We do not access or store your personal photos, files, or payment card details.

B. Information Automatically Processed

When you play Color Sort Master, certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic and gameplay information is stored locally on your device or processed through third-party services:

  • Gameplay Progress: Highest completed level, coin balance, earned stars, unlocked themes, and undo/hint consumable inventory. This data is saved locally on your device via Android Room Database / DataStore.
  • Device & Network Information: Device model, operating system version, general locale/language, and network state to ensure seamless performance.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Advertising ID (such as Google Play Services AAID) processed by Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play policies for ad delivery.

You can manage or reset your Advertising ID or opt out of interest-based ads anytime via your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

4. Device Permissions

Color Sort Master requests only the minimum permissions necessary to operate:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for retrieving advertisements from Google AdMob and checking updates.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to detect network connectivity before attempting to load ad units.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used to provide optional tactile haptic feedback when pouring balls or completing levels (can be toggled in game settings).
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used optionally on Android 13+ to notify you of daily challenge resets or streak reminders.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Color Sort Master is a general-audience casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). In our ad configurations, we request family-friendly ad ratings. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately so we can take necessary actions.

6. Data Retention and Security

Your puzzle progress and user preferences are retained locally on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear app data via Android settings, your local progress will be reset.

We implement industry-standard commercial security measures to protect any transmitted operational data. However, remember that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure and reliable.

7. Data Deletion and Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including:

  • The right to opt-out of personalized advertising.
  • The right to clear or delete all local game data by selecting Clear Storage / Clear Data in Android's Application Settings.
  • The right to inquire about data handled by third-party ad networks.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
